# Pixel Remote – Logoentwürfe, Runde 2

Erstellt mit dem eingebauten Imagegen-Tool. Ausgangspunkt: bevorzugte Blenden-Uhr (03) mit Gehäuseform der Objektiv-Uhr (01) und dem vom Nutzer bereitgestellten Foto seiner Fenix 8 als Formreferenz. Drei alternative Entwürfe, noch nicht in die Apps eingebaut.

Die abschließenden Korrekturprompts sind in [REFINEMENTS.md](REFINEMENTS.md) dokumentiert.

## Gemeinsamer Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Edit the logo into a new Pixel Remote app icon proposal. Reference 1 supplies the preferred six-blade mint camera aperture: preserve this aperture identity and large dark hexagonal center. Reference 2 supplies the more convincing wristwatch body, lugs and short strap segments: replace its lens with the aperture from reference 1. Reference 3 is the user's actual Fenix 8, use ONLY its rugged watch silhouette as a design reference, not the photograph, screen text, hand or background. Strong round bezel, angular lugs, sport-watch side pushbuttons rather than a single traditional winding crown. Front-facing centered flat graphic icon, not a product photo. Palette remains mint #9EE8C2, warm ivory and deep graphite #0C1210. Large readable geometric shapes with ample negative space and safe margins. Square opaque graphite background. No lettering, logos, numbers, clock hands, photo textures, realistic shading, mockup or extra symbols. Produce exactly one icon, not a grid.

## 01-fenix-klar

Variant A: the cleanest silhouette. Thick smooth ivory round bezel; simple angular ivory lugs connect two wide mint rounded strap tabs. Two compact side pushbuttons on the right and three subtle short pushbuttons on the left integrated close to the case. No bezel screw details or tick marks. Large mint aperture centered. Crisp simple and readily legible at small app-icon sizes.

## 02-fenix-outdoor

Variant B: strongest resemblance to the rugged Fenix 8 in the user's watch photo. Broad ivory bezel with four very simple dark screw dots at the diagonal quadrants, short notches at top and bottom, angular shoulders/lugs, short graphite-green strap tabs outlined in mint. Five compact sport-watch side pushbuttons, with a small mint accent on the upper right button. Keep details bold and sparse. The large mint camera aperture must remain the focal point. Graphic emblem, not a technical drawing.

## 03-fenix-kompakt

Variant C: compact integrated icon. A bold mint outer Fenix-style round case/bezel, ivory angular lugs and two short ivory strap segments with one broad graphite inset each. Clearly asymmetric sport pushbuttons: three on left, two on right, right upper button highlighted ivory. Separate the bezel from the large mint six-blade aperture with a broad dark gap; restrained thin ivory inner rim. No screws and no tick marks. Especially balanced silhouette and strong legibility.
